A phonetic approximation. Egyptian wrote consonants, not vowels, and some of our letters share one sign (C→K, O/U→W, V→F). So this spells your name by sound — the way souvenir cartouches do — not as an ancient scribe would have recorded it. Want the real rules? Start the lessons →
